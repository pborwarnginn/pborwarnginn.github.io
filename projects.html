01

Animal biometrics

Recognising the individual, beyond the breed.

Research on identifying individual dogs from facial images, including the MVITDog dataset and learning approaches for visually similar dogs.

Approach

Face-based recognition, dataset benchmarking, and contrastive learning for hard negative samples.

My contribution

Doctoral research on animal biometrics; first-author work on individual dog recognition.

Research output

MVITDog contains 300 individual dogs with at least 10 images per dog. The dataset benchmark is accepted / forthcoming; the hard-negative study is published in ECTI-CIT (2026).

02

Medical image analysis

Making clinical images measurable.

Collaborative research on tongue segmentation and motion analysis, chest X-ray interpretation, and visualisation for medical imaging.

Approach

Encoder–decoder networks, segmentation, optical flow, and deep learning for image analysis.

My contribution

Co-author on collaborative medical imaging studies with MVIT researchers and clinical collaborators.

Research output

Published studies span tongue segmentation, COVID-19 detection and heatmap generation, and cardiomegaly assessment.

03

Environmental monitoring

Reading the environment through cameras.

Using CCTV imagery for water-level detection, river-height prediction, and snow-scene segmentation.

Approach

Deep learning-based visual features for environmental image understanding.

My contribution

First-author research on water-level detection, river-height prediction, and snow-scene segmentation, in collaboration with Mahidol University and AIST.

Research output

Journal and conference publications on camera-based environmental monitoring.
