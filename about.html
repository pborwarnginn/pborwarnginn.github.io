About

Researcher & Lecturer

I am Punyanuch Borwarnginn, a researcher with the MVIT Research Lab and a lecturer at the Faculty of ICT, Mahidol University.

A little background.

I work at the intersection of computer vision, machine learning, and education. My research explores how visual information can support animal identification, medical image analysis, and environmental monitoring.

My doctoral research focused on animal biometrics using deep learning on images. Earlier work and international research experience have also shaped my interests in environmental monitoring, learning technologies, and applied computing.
